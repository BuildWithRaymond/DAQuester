# DAQuester 1.4.0 — A roomier planner and Altar Worship

**Run queue** is easier to find, with a play icon and a stronger ready state.
Fold the whole journey and queue into a compact bar to make room for quests.
The bar keeps your run button and progress visible, and expands with an animated
transition. Windows reduced-motion settings are respected.

The library now explains what you can select. Status icons, colors, row text and
tooltips distinguish ready quests, choices, cooldowns, completed goals and guides
for manual play. Disabled checkboxes explain why a quest cannot join the queue.
You can arrange eligible quests while disconnected; starting still needs a
connected character and checked requirements.

**Altar Worship** makes one Leather Shield offering at the Mileth Altar every
three hours, using a shield in your bag or buying one from Torrance for 750 gold.
The planner shows offerings made, the next due time and the remaining gold cost.
It stops after ten offerings. Only Priests can earn the legend mark; other classes
confirm once that they want the experience and ring instead. The first automated
offering is live-verified. The full ten-offering run and final reward checks have
synthetic coverage and still need a complete live run.

Saved **Spare a Stick** submissions can recover from matching recorded evidence
without another turn-in. Callough's repeat reward reply is recognized, and the
original completion time and eight-hour cooldown are preserved. If proof is still
missing, the queue stays paused and shows the reason.

Newly verified quest outcomes play the original blue spiral over the character.
A small notice shows completions for characters you aren't viewing. Old history
and duplicate updates stay quiet. Reduced motion uses a written notice. Original
game artwork is read from your supported installation and is not bundled.

Melee combat support selects a nearby monster, approaches and faces it, attacks,
uses suitable skills, and equips a melee weapon from the bag when needed. It is
currently available through the local control bridge while combat quests are
built. Adding a character now scrolls to the new card and focuses its name;
the character switcher lists names alphabetically. Enlarged sprite accents keep
their feet anchored on short cards.

Everything from 1.3.0 remains included: both Paulin flower quests and helper
characters, shared daily flower records, overlapping login launches, fast mode
on by default, and action-message/trade replay recording.

Download **DAQuester-win-Setup.exe** or **DAQuester-win-Portable.zip**. Both include
.NET, a short setup README and these notes. Keep the complete portable folder
together. Saved Data stays outside the package. Update checks default off;
checking, downloading and installing remain separate actions.

See the [setup guide](https://github.com/BuildWithRaymond/DAQuester/blob/v1.4.0/docs/GETTING_STARTED.md)
and [quest details](https://github.com/BuildWithRaymond/DAQuester/blob/v1.4.0/docs/QUESTS.md).
