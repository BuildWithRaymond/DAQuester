# DAQuester 1.7.0 — Woodland wolf and quest queue recovery

**Wolf of West Woodlands** now has an automatic recipe. It follows Throttler's
observed woodland conversation and route, handles the known entrance warning
even when it opens before the destination tile, and verifies **60,000 XP**, a
**Vanilla Potion or Fifleaf deum**, and the exact own Wolf legend. Reward proof
and final popup closure are saved separately. A fresh legend can reconcile a
manually completed quest when only an unsubmitted approach remains saved.
Unknown choices and uncertain submitted actions still require reconciliation.

**Wizardy research** prepares 30 Exkuranum, buying only the missing quantity at
Blackstar Village Research with fresh item and gold receipts. It continues from
the verified purchase after dismissing the merchant window and preserves that
return checkpoint across Resume. Research damage triggers a guarded potion dose
with actual inventory consumption and full-health proof; pending research
results remain independent of healing.

**Quest queues** recover from the reported movement interruptions: delayed
movement followed by a server refresh continues when no interruption is present;
Glioca's known landing uses the observed westward interior before NPC approach;
and a rejected step into a recorded doorway keeps the entrance available for
bounded retry instead of falsely reporting occupancy. Actual occupants, entry
refusals and uncertain movement still retain their safety gates. The observed
**Ciad greeting** is canceled during quest travel without advancing its quest.

**Spare a Stick** drops carried items named exactly Stick or Wooden Shield after
a verified automated outcome. Each drop is saved before submission and requires
its inventory receipt. Equipped items, Oak Stick and other names are retained.
Scheduler shows the observed Callough mark and offers an explicit **Try for more
marks** decision. Each obtained outcome stops repeats until approved again;
approval preserves prior evidence and the eight-hour cooldown and does not turn
Scheduler on by itself.

The new recovery paths have synthetic regression coverage. Wolf discovery and
player-completed rewards, and the Exkuranum purchase, are observed; a fresh full
automatic Wolf run, potion healing, and the complete corrected research/queue
journeys remain unqualified live. Higher numbered automatic Callough progression
also still needs live qualification.

Download **DAQuester-win-Setup.exe** or **DAQuester-win-Portable.zip**. Both include
the .NET runtime and pinned DAWindower X. Keep all portable files together.
Saved Data stays outside the package. Update checks default off; checking,
downloading and installing remain separate player actions. Installing an update
requires the existing safe-exit checks and does not close game clients.
