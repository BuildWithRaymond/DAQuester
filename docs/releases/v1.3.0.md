# DAQuester 1.3.0 — Sevti flowers and helper characters

**Cured the Sick Child** now joins the quest library. Pick a Sevti Blossom at
Mehadi each day, or use your other saved characters to pick flowers, bring them
to Paulin's room, trade them to the quester and log back out. Every turn-in gives
the flower with 5,000 coins so it reaches the child. Helpers can finish the flower
run in one day; characters that cannot pick yet show when they picked or were
refused.

**Aided the Pact of Anaman** follows the same route with Golden Blossoms and the
same helper option. Give each flower plainly to Paulin for the chief physician,
without gold. Its brown legend mark is hidden from other players; the quest
explains this before you start. Only one of these two legend marks can be earned
on a character. A character's daily flower allowance is shared across both quests.

Launch remains available while another character logs in. Overlapping launches
open their windows immediately and enter saved logins one at a time. Fast mode
defaults on for every character, and your own connected characters no longer
slow each other's walking. Other nearby players still do.

Clicking the ground or your own character to focus a game window no longer pauses
automation. Clicking an NPC, monster or another player still pauses it, and the
diagnostics log names the character and clicked target. Replays include both
sides of trades and server trade updates. Game action messages remain in replays
and diagnostics; chat, world shouts and popups are excluded.

Quest completion still requires observed evidence. Existing quest policies,
connection isolation, durable checkpoints and reconciliation remain protected.
Update checks default off; checking, downloading and installing require separate
actions. Packages preserve your saved Data and include the .NET runtime.

Download **DAQuester-win-Setup.exe** or **DAQuester-win-Portable.zip**. Keep the
complete portable folder together. See the
[quest guide](https://github.com/BuildWithRaymond/DAQuester/blob/v1.3.0/docs/QUESTS.md)
for flower requirements and helper behavior.
