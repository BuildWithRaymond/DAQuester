# DAQuester 1.0.6 — A new quest planner

This update replaces the old journal layout with a quest planner. Choose your
character, pick quests from the library, and drag the queue into the order you
want. The current journey stays beside the library, with saved progress and
recent activity close at hand.

![DAQuester 1.0.6 quest planner](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BuildWithRaymond/DAQuester/v1.0.6/docs/screenshots/planner.png)

## Your queue, saved for each character

- Drag quests to reorder them, or use the move buttons and Ctrl+Up/Down.
  Remove entries individually or clear the queue.
- Each entry runs one verified attempt. Quest cooldowns and repeat rules still
  apply. Pause retains the order; Resume checks saved work before continuing.
- Switch characters from the header. Their queues, history, and progress stay
  separate.
- Save a character's connected location with **Set home here**, then opt into
  **Return home when finished**. Arrival must be confirmed before it's done.

## Characters and quest availability

Each saved character now has its own **Launch** button. With a saved password,
DAQuester uses DAOrganizer's owned-window and verified login-form approach to
sign into that character. Without one, you can sign in manually. Passwords stay
encrypted by Windows.

Observed levels and your character's own legend marks are now remembered across
restarts. A finished Muisir, Birth, or final Emerald legend shows **Completed**,
and the Suomi legend ends Hairstyle attempts. **Spare a Stick is only selectable
at levels 1–11**, including when the known level is saved offline. Existing
pending transactions can still be reconciled.

Ready and Choices rows have a soft green tint. Blocked and unavailable entries
are dimmed, with a reason you can inspect. The doubled search placeholder is
fixed. Windows, dialogs, menus, and scrollbars share the dark theme.

![Saved characters with individual launch actions](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BuildWithRaymond/DAQuester/v1.0.6/docs/screenshots/characters.png)

## Two more automatic quests

**Emerald Dawn** searches Shinewood Entrance for an insignia, then follows your
Knights, Necromancer, or sale choice. You can visit Gans before delivery. The
search sweeps reachable entrance tiles, saves each dialogue checkpoint, and
checks recovery and delivery against your own legends and inventory. Unexpected
pages pause for review.

**Shinewood Faerie** tries for a pet for any class and learns eligible missing
Priest/Wizard spells during the visit. The greeting now works with the Faerie's
actual map-reactor dialogue. If no faerie will bond, the observed one-deoch wait
is saved. Return travel tries town songs, then ready dachaidh, then walking when
you have acknowledged the warning. Saved refusals and returns reconcile without
another adoption or escape command.

The library now contains 71 entries and six automatic recipes. Other entries
remain guides for manual play.

## Travel and recovery

- Departures from Suomi can use Keane's ferry when the recorded route goes
  through Abel. Travel choices come from the live dialogue, and routes replan
  from the actual arrival.
- Long quest actions publish checkpoints as they happen. Ground collection
  uses fast walking, responds to newly visible loot, and settles movement
  before pickup. Player proximity still slows movement.
- Discovery popups and insignia Next pages survive early inventory updates.
  Unanswered submissions remain pending instead of being sent again.
- Faerie travel recognizes Rucesion Threshold as a safe landing and recognizes
  numeric spell-level suffixes in known spells and dachaidh.

The README and documentation are reorganized, old task plans and the retired
HTML concept are removed, and screenshots now show the native planner.

## Updating from an older version

In **Settings**, click **Check for updates**, then **Download update**. Finish
your quests and close DAQuester-launched game clients before choosing **Install
and exit**. Saved data stays outside the package. Startup checks remain optional
and off by default; checking never downloads or installs automatically.

For a fresh install, use **DAQuester-win-Setup.exe**. The portable ZIP and
SHA-256 checksums are also attached to this release.

Verified with **670 synthetic tests**, native UI checks, and release package
verification. Public 1.0.0 and 1.0.5 apps detect 1.0.6; the new app reports no
further update. Public files match their SHA-256 checksums and GitHub digests.
Verification uses synthetic characters and packets, never real character actions.
