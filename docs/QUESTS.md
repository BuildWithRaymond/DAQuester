# Quest details and observed behavior

**Fisk's Secret** follows the secret-preserving path with missing-only supplies,
observed payments and item receipts. The guide lists level 21+ provisionally.
Observed spending was **32,600 gold**, followed by **750,000 XP** and the own
secrecy legend. After the paid bouquet acceptance, the saved one-hour wait yields
to other ready queued quests. Otherwise the character waits at a free Suomi
Tavern tile **(10–14,1), facing down**. A full row leaves the paid wait intact.
An hour allows a check; only a new Special Bouquet proves collection. A not-ready
reply schedules another check without paying again.

**Cap / Band of Danaan** share one queue and journal identity. The available
route is **Donnan/Cail**, with an explicit per-character acceptance of Greim's
**supplied-Mold rite risk**. The guide lists level 50+ provisionally. Observed
spending was **6,620 gold**, followed by **1,000,000 XP**, one Cap and its own
worthy legend. Current equipment and bag receipts handle armed/unarmed shrine
access; actual menu consent handles Path Reception and monastery entry. Final
Next, item, XP notice, own legend and popup close all require proof. The actual
Cap/Band item must match its legend; unknown variant dialogue pauses. No
consecration, reversion or other Avatar route runs automatically.

**Shinewood Faerie** visits the Faerie near (71,37) in Shinewood Forest 11 and
says **Hello**. Every class may adopt one **Pet Faerie**. Existing carried or
equipped pets and verified adoption receipts prevent duplicate adoption.
Requires **level 11+** to pass Shinewood Forest 7, as reported by the player.
Reserve one free inventory slot and five weight if a pet is still needed.

During the same visit, priests learn missing **deo lamh** at level 11 and
**creag neart** at level 20. Master priests may also learn **deo searg gar**;
pure master priests may learn **deo searg**. Master wizards may learn
**fas spiorad**; pure master wizards may learn **Wings of Protection**.
Fresh class evidence plans the goals, and the Faerie's actual offers resolve
unknown eligibility. Only actual inventory/spellbook updates prove rewards.
Unknown choices and ambiguous submissions pause with their pages preserved.

Bring a town song/scroll or ready **dachaidh**; Shinewood can be dangerous at
lower levels. Without a usable escape, a fully dark warning appears before
departure. **Cancel** is the default. **Continue and walk back** requires a
verified collision route and explicitly accepts the dangerous walking return.
Reconnect requires a fresh acknowledgment. Recall Scroll is excluded because
it returns to the last death location rather than a town.

If no faerie bonds, the observed reply asks for a **one-deoch wait**. The runner
records that failed attempt, closes the reply, finishes any eligible spells and
returns. The journal reports the wait without claiming a pet reward or guessing
a real-world retry date. A saved refusal also reconciles after a manual close or
return without repeating adoption or consuming another song.

The successful bond offers **250hp and 250mp**, followed by **Are you willing?**
The runner selects the offered Yes only after that exact fresh Faerie offer.
It saves this separate bond submission before sending, verifies the new Pet
Faerie, and finishes dialogue before returning. The observed success delivers
the item and closes dialogue without a final text page. A remaining submitted
popup can be closed after the item receipt; an uncertain bond never repeats Yes.

After rewards or a definitive refusal, the runner uses a town song first, dachaidh with at least 30 mana
second, and walking exits last. Item/spell slots are resolved again at send time.
Walking follows documented doors and actual server landings back to Mileth.
A missing teleport reply preserves the return checkpoint without consuming a
second song or casting again. Reward receipts remain saved if departure fails.
Resume reconciles them before continuing. Explicit later visits check newly
eligible missing spells; there is no repeat timer. Rucesion Song's observed
Rucesion Threshold landing is accepted as a safe return. Spell matching accepts
the observed numeric level suffix while preserving raw spellbook evidence.
Pet-failure and non-Priest/Wizard dialogues are captured; successful pet adoption,
Priest/Wizard learning and a full automated live visit still need validation.
Other quest conversations and all test transports remain synthetic.

**Emerald Dawn** is available in the selector and library. Requires level 11+.

starting; the dialog lists each path's legend marks and gold. The choice can be
changed with **Change choices** until the insignia is handed over. Gans (the
Druid line) is not offered: he neither takes the insignia nor rewards anything.
Verified live 2026-10-11: Emerald Knights gave the "Aided the Emerald Knights"
mark and no gold; Necromancer Order gave 20,000 gold plus "Aided the
Necromancer Order"; Rogue Order paid 1,000,000 gold and no mark.

The runner visits Mioas in Suomi Tavern (Adventures), speaks with Berg (Emerald
Knights), collects one Emerald Knight Insignia in Shinewood Entrance, then returns
to Berg and selects the guide's recovery option. A fresh recovery legend and

guide are supported, and the exact observed mark is saved. Carried insignia skips
collection; an existing recovery legend with an insignia skips both introductions.

actual XP changes, without guessing level-based reward amounts. Existing final
faction marks finish without another delivery. Emerald Dawn never repeats.

**Cured the Sick Child** is available in the selector and library. Requires level
11+ and 5,000 gold per flower. Paulin (Loures Cyril Corridor, map 3070) needs five
Sevti Blossoms, one per visit; the flower is the White Blossom picked by stepping
onto (2,9) in Mehadi Heart West (map 3077). Each character can pick one flower a
day; Paulin takes any number on the same day (verified live 2026-10-11). The mark
locks out "Aided the Pact of Anaman" and the other way round; only the legend mark
blocks, never partial progress.

Before starting, choose **One flower a day** (only this character picks) or
**Bring flowers from my other characters**. Helpers come from the saved character
list: DAQuester logs them in through the launch queue, picks at Mehadi, walks them
next to the quester in Paulin's room and trades the flower over, then logs out the
helpers it logged in. Helpers already online stay online. Whenever a flower falls
through (a refused pick, a failed login, a helper you take over), the next checked
helper starts at once; a failed login is retried once, and a helper launched by
hand meanwhile is used as it is. A helper you take over is never logged out.
Fewer helpers than needed is allowed; the rest follow on later days. Set **Flowers already given to Paulin**
for turn-ins made by hand, since the server shows no count.

Each turn-in always chooses "Give Sevti Blossom and 5000 coins" and is proven by
exactly 5,000 gold and one White Blossom gone plus the server's "150000
experience!" message. A trade is proven only by the flower arriving in the
quester's inventory; "You exchanged." also follows the first OK. A refused pick
("You notice a small white flower that has been picked") is recorded and shown;
DAQuester expects that character can pick again 24 hours after its last pick. The
quest completes when the own legend shows "Cured a sick child in Loures". The fifth
turn-in also sends "A sick child recovers because of you". Verified live 2026-10-12
on valadriel: Ferda, swallow and Disillusion picked, traded and were logged out
automatically, and the legend appeared after the fifth flower.

**Aided the Pact of Anaman** runs on the same engine as Cured the Sick Child, with
the Tulsi Blossom instead of the Sevti. Walking onto (11,3) in Mehadi Heart West
opens "You see a flower here." and "You see a flower here with seven green leaves
and blue center." before "Pick the flower"; the item is the **Golden Blossom**.
Paulin: "The sick child, how is she?", "Offer Tulsi Blossom", Next, then always the
plain "Give Tulsi Blossom", which hands it to the chief physician, who is in on the
pact. It costs no gold. Proof is one Golden Blossom gone with gold unchanged, plus
the experience message; whatever page follows the give is logged and closed. The
coins option ("Give Tulsi Blossom and 5000 coins") sends it secretly to the child
instead: on 2026-10-12 kullan gave five that way, each paying 10,000 experience,
and earned no legend mark, so those turn-ins don't count toward the pact. The
legend is "Aided the Pact of Anaman" after five plain gives: a brown mark, written
"- Aided the Pact of Anaman - Deoch …", that other players can't see. Verified
live 2026-10-12 on heartc with four helpers: each plain give paid "5000
experience!" (Vorlof says 10,000) and Paulin "takes the blossom and thanks you";
the fourth also brought "A faint but palpable shadow looms near you". One pick a day is shared across every flower: zemial, after picking
a Tulsi, was refused at the Sevti spot. The Tulsi spot refuses with "You notice a
green leaved plant that has been picked"; the Sevti spot with "You notice a small
white flower that has been picked". DAQuester keeps one pick record per
character for both quests. The swamp can also blind for about 17 seconds ("All
goes black" … "You can see again.").

**Altar Worship** drops an item worth more than 500 gold on the Mileth Altar (map
500) every 3 hours; Vorlof reports the mark after 10 such offerings. DAQuester
buys a Leather Shield from Torrance (Mileth Armor Shop, map 133, entity at (3,5)):

offering is answered with "25 experience!" and "You feel a touch of light around
you" (type 3) while the shield leaves the bag; that pair is the proof. Verified

run it for the experience after confirming once. Only the first offering has run
live through DAQuester (Ferda, 2026-10-12); a full 10-offering run has not been
live-tested, so the 3-hour waits, the 10th offering and the reward check are
proven by tests only.

## Current operational limits

Known obtainable supplies are prepared before quest NPC visits. A queue combines
the next ready quests' consumable needs, subtracts the actual carried inventory,
and prepares a fitting prefix while reserving space for equipment and rewards.
Recorded costs limit optional future purchases; fresh shop quotes authorize
payment, and the current quest's known mandatory payment funds stay reserved.
Shared consumables add together, including handoffs whose purchase stage was
already completed. Dialogue-selected branches retain their own requirements;
unknown branches, timed pickups, acceptance gates, and stage-produced items do
not authorize speculative collection or spending.

Danaan prepares its shared Gland, Mold, fruit, and Leather Greaves bundle early.
Gland hunting can use reachable Mehadi sources instead of a fixed Enchanted
Garden trip. The Mehadi locations are guide claims; actual named inventory
receipts still prove each pickup. Mundane approaches stop when the intended NPC
is within ten tile steps and visible, after validated doorway departure. Cached
identity alone is insufficient. Both captured Donnan referrals are supported;
the [captured-run audit](research/danaan-captured-run-audit.md) records coverage.
Undine's known popup or active
status weapon warning stops travel until both equipment and bag receipts confirm
removal. The shrine armament rules remain separate.

Hunting can bash with bare hands; no equipped or carried melee weapon is
required. An owned usable melee weapon is still equipped when available. Only
an actual equip submission without confirmation pauses combat for inspection.
Ceannlaidir's equipped-weapon entry requirement remains separate.

Preparation, including an uncertain purchase or pickup, has its own durable
checkpoint and preserves the quest's original dialogue position. These changes
have synthetic regression coverage; no new live automatic run is certified.

Three characters completed Spare a Stick in game. Nuclear received Stick,
Wooden Shield and 3,500 XP; Clock received Oak Stick, Wooden Shield and 3,500 XP,
with permanent completion. Awesome's corrected patrol run received 3,500 XP,
50 gold, Stick and Wooden Shield. Runs included manual pauses and reconnects.
The latest completion variants are replay-tested. Automatic excess-gold banking,
cooldown refusal and live player detours await live validation. Perfect Hairstyle's
manually assisted Nuclear run and fully automated Clock attempt are captured.
Both received Emerald Ring/15,000 XP; their weekly legend goals remain unfinished.
Mundane Birth completed live on Nuclear and Clock. Ambiguous submissions pause without resending.
Saved passwords allow automatic sign-in through DAQuester's Launch actions;
manual sign-in remains available. The account manager edits character names and
stores optional passwords encrypted for the current Windows account.
