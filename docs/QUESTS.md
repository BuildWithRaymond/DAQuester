# Quest details and observed behavior

**Shinewood Faerie** visits the Faerie near (71,37) in Shinewood Forest 11 and
says **Hello**. Every class may adopt one **Pet Faerie**. Existing carried or
equipped pets and verified adoption receipts prevent duplicate adoption.
Requires **level 11+** to pass Shinewood Forest 7, as reported by the player.
Reserve one free inventory slot and five weight if a pet is still needed.

During the same visit, priests learn missing **deo lamh** at level 11 and
**creag neart** at level 20. Master priests may also learn **deo searg gar**;
pure master priests may learn **deo searg**. Master wizards may learn
**fas spiorad**; pure master wizards may learn **Wings of Protection**.
Fresh class evidence plans the goals, and the Faerie's actual offers resolve
unknown eligibility. Only actual inventory/spellbook updates prove rewards.
Unknown choices and ambiguous submissions pause with their pages preserved.

Bring a town song/scroll or ready **dachaidh**; Shinewood can be dangerous at
lower levels. Without a usable escape, a fully dark warning appears before
departure. **Cancel** is the default. **Continue and walk back** requires a
verified collision route and explicitly accepts the dangerous walking return.
Reconnect requires a fresh acknowledgment. Recall Scroll is excluded because
it returns to the last death location rather than a town.

records that failed attempt, closes the reply, finishes any eligible spells and
returns. The journal reports the wait without claiming a pet reward or guessing
a real-world retry date. A saved refusal also reconciles after a manual close or
return without repeating adoption or consuming another song.
