# Ordinary Spare a Stick completion

An ordinary reward completes the current attempt while leaving permanent
completion unset. Every verified attempt saves its server reply time and an
eight-hour next-attempt deadline, independently of the Repeat checkbox.

Retain validated Callough replies and correlated inventory/experience snapshots
in the runner's bounded evidence history. Checkpoint the reply before awaiting
later reward packets. After interruption, reconcile observations under the same
runner ownership gate; later packets may complete a paused transaction without
movement, dialog commands, another submission, or resuming automation.

Require the recorded turn-in, its generation and later revisions, an exact
calibrated Callough completion reply, known inventory, six branches consumed and
experience gained. Preserve unresolved transactions. Other quest policies and
permanent completion take precedence. Deriving success from generic rewards or
automatically resubmitting would weaken these boundaries and is excluded.

Show a verified ordinary attempt as completed for now, with the next deadline
visible even after restoring an offline checkpoint. Test interruption, later
packets, reply persistence, generation isolation, partial rewards and single-run
cooldown storage. Run the full suite and native UI checks before packaging.

Historical transactions without a saved reply remain pending unless the player
explicitly confirms completion and supplies its time for a manual repair. Label
that confirmation separately; never fabricate a server dialog or timestamp.
