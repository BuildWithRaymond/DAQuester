# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Welcome newcomers, explain technical
tradeoffs plainly, and focus criticism on the work. Harassment, threats,
discrimination, doxxing and publishing private credentials or player information
are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, limit participation or close discussions
that violate these expectations. Security-sensitive reports should use the
private channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). These expectations apply to the
repository, issues, pull requests and project discussions.
